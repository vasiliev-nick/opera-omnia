Nick (2026-10-23) about the paragraphs after Def. ∅.43 (convergence in mean / mean square, §∅.10, scratchpad p2/src/b10.html): «текста стало больше раза в 4, но согласись, насколько он хорошо теперь читается! Давай этого языка придерживаться в будущем».
His own diagnosis of WHY it is good (the core rule): it does not bombard the reader with terms and «согласно такой-то теореме» inside one paragraph; it gives the general intuition in plain Russian, says why we do this, carries the motivation inside the text, and lays things out conceptually, showing why it is really so, instead of stern formalism and forwarding between earlier statements. Formulas and references are there, but they do not dominate and do not obscure the essence.
Techniques the model passage uses (hints, not a checklist): explain the name and what is measured; the simplest familiar case (equal probabilities: integral = arithmetic mean); familiar words (RMSE, «среднеквадратичное отклонение»); compare with neighbouring notions by what each one watches; name the surprising consequence outright, words first, then a short derivation.
**Why:** he reads such text easily and with pleasure; terse definition-then-lemma text drains him (same root as [[no-dense-paragraphs]] and the anti-drudgery rule).
**How to apply:** write every new notion in all books this way (recorded in textbook/FORMAT.md «Текст», «Язык разъяснений»); several-fold longer text is fine; keep definitions and proofs formal next to it. When editing older chapters, bring bare definitions up to this level. Also fits chat answers ([[chat-explanation-style]]).
