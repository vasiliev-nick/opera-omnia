2026-10-23: Nick asked «выделим часть про случайные величины как векторы в отдельную главу»; I wrote a full 8-section chapter ∠ ([[textbook-ch-angle-state]]). He liked it but said he meant a section within a chapter (like §∅.1, §∅.2), and accepted the result («написал и написал»).
**Why:** a whole chapter costs far more work and reading time than he expected; a section would have been enough.
**How to apply:** when Nick asks to split material out or add a new unit, default to a § inside the existing chapter, and say the planned size (number of sections, figures) in the first line of the plan before writing; only build a new chapter when he names one explicitly or confirms.
