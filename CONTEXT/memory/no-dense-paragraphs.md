Nick (2026-10-22, about the paragraph after Def. ∅.43 «Предел единствен…» in §∅.10): even from a distance he could see it was a solid run of formal transitions and lemma references, where it could have been normal Russian prose giving motivation and explanation. «Не пиши такие супер-плотные абзацы… всё то же самое можно перефразировать по-человечески (пусть оно занимает больше места, зато так понятнее)».
**Why:** dense chains like «по лемме X … по следствию Y … значит …» are hard to read and kill his motivation (same root as the anti-drudgery rule).
**How to apply:** in every book, a main-text paragraph says first why and what we want to see, then argues in words; one idea per paragraph; formulas and lemma numbers only where needed; bookkeeping goes into (foldable) proofs or separate displays. Longer is fine. Recorded in textbook/FORMAT.md «Текст». When editing any chapter, rewrite such paragraphs on sight. See [[chat-explanation-style]], [[textbook-ch0-state]].
